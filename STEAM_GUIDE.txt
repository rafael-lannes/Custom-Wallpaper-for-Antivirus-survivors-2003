3. Extract the ZIP anywhere on your computer (no installation needed).

[h3]Step 2: Choose Your Custom Image[/h3]
1. Run [b]CustomWallpaper.exe[/b].
2. The game package path ([i]AVS03Pro.pck[/i]) is auto-detected. If your game is installed in a custom Steam library folder, click [b]Browse...[/b] and locate it.
3. Click [b]Choose...[/b] and select any image from your computer.
4. Check the live preview box to make sure you like how it looks.

[h3]Step 3: Apply to Game[/h3]
1. Click the blue [b]✓ Apply Wallpaper to Game[/b] button.
2. A success popup will confirm that the wallpaper was patched into the game files.

[h3]Step 4: Launch and Enjoy![/h3]
Open [b]Antivirus Survivors 2003 Professional[/b] on Steam. Your new wallpaper will immediately appear on the desktop and home screen!

[h2]How to Restore the Original Wallpaper[/h2]
If you ever want to revert back to the default unmodded wallpaper:
1. Open [b]CustomWallpaper.exe[/b].
2. Click [b]Restore Original (Backup)[/b].
3. Confirm the prompt and the game file will be restored immediately.
